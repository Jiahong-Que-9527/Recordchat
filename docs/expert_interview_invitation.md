# ONE Record practitioner interview invitation

Use this message for the first external validation round. Personalize the
opening and closing, but keep the scope and privacy commitments unchanged.

## English invitation

> Hi [Name],
>
> I am running a small validation round for **RecordChat**, an independent,
> citation-first assistant for IATA ONE Record concepts, ontology, JSON-LD,
> API ideas, and NE:ONE implementation guidance. It is not an official IATA
> product.
>
> Would you be open to a **10–20 minute** session? I would ask you to try
> 2–3 real ONE Record questions from your work, then briefly judge whether the
> answer, its citations, and its practical usefulness make sense. If useful,
> I also have two short fallback questions so sessions are comparable.
>
> This is product research, not a sales call. I will not record audio or
> video, and I will keep the notes anonymous (for example, `P01` rather than
> your name or company). Please do not share confidential material, customer
> data, or internal logs; a paraphrased question is perfect.
>
> If you are interested, could you reply with 2–3 time windows that work for
> you between [date range]? I will send a short one-screen scope note before
> the session.
>
> Thank you — your practical feedback would directly help identify where the
> assistant is trustworthy and where it needs improvement.

## Facilitator notes

- Keep the final sentence truthful: do not state that recording or named
  attribution is available unless the participant explicitly changes the
  agreed session terms.
- Use the exact fallback prompts and score sheet in
  [expert_session_script.md](expert_session_script.md).
- Store only the anonymous participant ID in session notes and do not copy
  personal details into request-log annotations.
